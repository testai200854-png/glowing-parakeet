HKDSE Chemistry Syllabus & Exam Structure

Exam overview, paper distribution, and syllabus checklist at a glance.

Reference the structure of Paper 1 and Paper 2 while keeping track of compulsory and elective topic coverage for HKDSE Chemistry.

⏱️

Exam Overview

  • Paper 1 (Compulsory): 2.5 hours, 120 marks — Section A (MC, 36 marks) & Section B (Structured, 84 marks). Weighting: 60%.
  • Paper 2 (Elective): 1 hour, 40 marks — Structured questions on two elective topics. Weighting: 20%.
  • SBA (School-based Assessment): Practical work performance. Weighting: 20%.
