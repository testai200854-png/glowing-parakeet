Q1 4 marks

Which conjugate base is produced when HCl dissolves in water?

General MCQ Ch14-19 Acids and Bases MCQ pattern
The correct answer is Cl⁻ because HCl donates a proton to water, leaving the chloride ion as its conjugate base.
Common mistake: confusing the conjugate base with H₂O or writing H₃O⁺ instead of Cl⁻.
Q2 6 marks

Explain why the C=C bond in ethene is more reactive than the C–C bond in ethane.

Structural Ch20-22 Fossil Fuels & Carbon Compounds Short response
Mention the π bond in ethene; it has higher electron density and is easier to attack than the σ bond in ethane.
Common mistake: stating only bond length or strength without distinguishing σ and π bond character.
Q3 10 marks

Describe how limiting reagent and percentage yield are used to evaluate reaction efficiency in industrial chemistry.

Essay practice Ch10-13 Metals Essay
Cover definitions of limiting reagent, calculation of actual yield from stoichiometry, and use of percentage yield to compare efficiency.
Common mistake: failing to identify the limiting reagent or using the theoretical yield instead of actual yield in percentage yield calculations.
Q4 5 marks

Outline the procedure for preparing a standard solution by titration and state one key control variable.

Practical Ch14-19 Acids and Bases Short response
Include rinsing the burette, precise volume readings, and control of the titre until the end-point is reached.
Common mistake: not describing the need to rinse equipment or not identifying the control variable such as temperature or concentration.
